# Oracle Lunar — Release Notes

## 2026-08-05 — Feature film, story front matter & bullet-proof playback

**SCAM THE SCAMMER — JUZZY STYLE (5 min feature)**
- The finished film is rendered and live in the Media Library: 4:59 runtime, 1920x1080,
  30fps, H.264 video + AAC stereo, faststart-optimised (~37 MB) for instant streaming.
- 38 scenes with Ken Burns motion, ElevenLabs narration and an original synth-funk score
  sidechain-ducked to -14 LUFS for YouTube loudness compliance.
- Stored privately per user and registered as a library tile with a generated poster frame.

**Story Writer front matter**
- Optional **Prelude** and **Dedication** sections, each writable by hand or generated by Oracle.
- Both are exported as proper EPUB3 front matter, ahead of chapter one.
- **Oracle Takeover** finishes every incomplete chapter in the author's own voice.

**Playback resilience**
- The 59-step recovery ladder now has a final rung: when every retry fails, the player
  shows **Download MP4**, **Open in new tab** and **Retry** instead of a frozen frame.
- Applies app-wide to signed media, the Media Library, Movie Studio and Living GIFs.

**Library sharing**
- Finished videos carry a share link and a fault-tolerant download button
  (blob save → direct anchor → new tab), with a toast for every outcome.
