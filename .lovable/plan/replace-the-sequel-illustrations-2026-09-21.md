# Replace the sequel illustrations

## Goal
Replace the 24 repetitive chapter pictures in **SCAM THE SCAMMER II — PAYBACK PROTOCOL (OZZY STYLE)** with one unique, highly detailed, photorealistic action scene per chapter.

## What will change
- Read each chapter and choose its strongest real action or cliffhanger moment.
- Build 24 distinct scene briefs matching the chapter’s location, characters, wardrobe, vehicles, lighting, and events.
- Use the Juzzy and Sienna appearance established by the cover for continuity, without repeating the same pose or setting.
- Include exactly one romantic kiss scene, only where it fits the story.
- Generate one full-page, Kindle-suitable image per chapter with realistic anatomy, faces, lighting, debris, motion, and environmental detail.
- Replace each chapter’s current image and keep its placement aligned with the illustrated moment.
- Leave the approved front and rear covers unchanged.
- Remove the superseded chapter image files only after all replacements are generated, saved, and verified.

## Quality checks
- Confirm all 24 chapters have exactly one new image and a valid placement.
- Confirm scenes are visibly varied and correspond to their chapter text.
- Confirm only one image contains a kiss.
- Confirm the EPUB builder can embed all 24 replacements.
