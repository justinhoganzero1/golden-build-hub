# Oracle-only experience

## What changes
- After sign-in, Oracle becomes the main screen instead of the dashboard: the fictional ad presenter fills the available space, with small microphone, sound, typing and account controls.
- Keep every existing feature and saved item. Open supported tools, Library, account settings and finished creations over Oracle, with a clear return to the conversation. Preserve existing direct links as compatibility paths, not the normal starting experience.
- Keep Reimagined separate and unchanged. Sign-in, membership, payments and legal screens remain available when required.

## Lifelike presenter without generating each response
- Use a private-to-the-interface catalogue of approved presenter clips: listening, acknowledging, thinking, reacting and waiting for a creation. Users do not browse this internal catalogue; their own Library remains available through Oracle.
- Select footage by conversation state and appropriate tone, avoiding recently played clips where alternatives exist. Use smooth transitions, fixed framing and reduced-data playback on phones.
- Start with suitable existing footage only. Do not use unrelated advertising scenes as simulated listening or manufacture motion by presenting a frozen image as video. Where approved footage is insufficient, use an honest portrait fallback until suitable clips are supplied or separately approved for production.
- Speak new answers with device voices by default. Only play a recorded spoken phrase when that exact phrase fits; do not imply prerecorded lip movements match arbitrary new speech.
- Identify the presenter as AI with reused footage through a compact persistent label and further detail in its information control. Do not pretend a prerecorded response is live-generated video or a human call.
- Finite clips can eventually repeat. Avoid immediate repeats, but do not promise infinitely unique responses or flawless lip sync.

## Oracle's answers and background work
- Use the member's saved history and relevant private Library records for personal context. Use existing live web research for current facts, showing sources and distinguishing saved material from newly researched information.
- Choose sources by relevance, recency when needed and credibility. A thumbs-up helps personalise future choices; it is not evidence that a factual claim is true.
- Keep supported creation jobs running while the presenter remains visible. Report real progress, errors and save outcomes; ask “Would you like to see it?” only after a genuine finished result exists.
- Reveal finished products over Oracle and return to the same conversation. Preserve existing isolated app viewing and private-media access protections.
- Unsupported actions or disconnected services receive a truthful explanation, not a fabricated successful result. Purchases, sends and other consequential actions retain existing approval requirements.

## A growing library, without hidden charges
- Save clip metadata, playback history and feedback separately from answer memory and member-created media. Never mix one member's private content into another's answers.
- Refresh approved clip availability and learn preferences from actual interactions. Do not continuously generate videos, scrape arbitrary footage, or run paid background research without a user request.
- Replaying an existing clip avoids a new generation fee, but initial production, hosting and data still have costs. New AI answers, live research and requested creations keep existing wallet charging and the required margin. Premium voice remains an explicit paid choice.
- No proxies, automatic paid avatar sessions or promises that the entire AI app is free.

## Technical approach
- Retain the canonical Oracle conversation engine, authentication and membership guards. Add a presentation layer and focused tool adapters rather than deleting modules or mounting a second chat engine.
- Separate deterministic clip selection from playback, speech and creation-job state. Bound preloading and recent-history tracking; stop playback on unmount and respect explicit sound/microphone gestures.
- Reuse existing research, saved conversations, creation services and artifact viewer. Resolve private media when opened and resume known jobs without replaying uncertain paid actions.
- Add small tests for anti-repeat selection, truthful fallback, no paid speech in device mode, access enforcement, account isolation and completion-before-reveal. Any new stored feedback/catalogue data must have authenticated, least-privilege access.
- Deliver in increments: Oracle-first shell and tool access; approved clip playback and selection; source/feedback integration; background-result and regression checks. Retain working compatibility paths throughout.

## Verification and release
- Test signed-in use on the current 384 × 623 phone size and desktop: presenter framing, typing, mic/sound controls, returning from tools and account access.
- Verify real conversation, cited research and a supported creation through completion, reveal, Library save and reopening. Exercise failure and insufficient-funds paths without retrying uncertain payments.
- Verify original modules and Reimagined still open correctly, and signed-out or ineligible users cannot bypass access through the new screen.
- Report actual speech/video limitations and any paths not tested. Do not publish until Juzzy explicitly requests publication.

## Limits to agree on
This provides a real interactive Oracle conversation with reused presenter footage—not unlimited free live video generation. A varied, convincing clip catalogue depends on having enough suitable footage; new clip production needs separate cost approval.
